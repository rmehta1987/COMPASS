1 · Find a variable

One construct at a time, as a bare noun phrase. This maps a described construct onto instrument items — it is not a search engine for relationships, and a question naming two constructs will silently match only one of them (measured: environment and hypertension returned five hypertension items and no environment item, without abstaining). Skip "show me questions related to…": measured, that wrapper costs 0.11–0.27 cosine and can push a good hit below the abstention threshold.

Works (measured cosine): hypertension 0.94 · age at first menstrual period 0.97 · breast cancer diagnosis 0.94 · type 2 diabetes diagnosis 0.92 · household income 0.90 · cigarette smoking frequency 0.84 · neighborhood environment 0.86. Abstains (below 0.729, correctly): age at cancer diagnosis 0.66 · air pollution exposure 0.68.
